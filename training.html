📚 Module 1: Pressure Measurement

Pressure is defined as force per unit area. It's one of the most commonly measured process variables.

Common Pressure Units:

UnitAbbreviationApproximate Equivalent
PascalPa1 N/m²
Barbar100,000 Pa
Pounds per Square Inchpsi6,895 Pa
Atmosphereatm101,325 Pa

Pressure Reference Types:

  • Absolute Pressure: Measured relative to perfect vacuum
  • Gauge Pressure: Measured relative to atmospheric pressure
  • Differential Pressure: The difference between two pressures
  • Vacuum Pressure: Pressures below atmospheric

📚 Module 2: 4-20mA Current Loops

The 4-20mA current loop is the most common analog signal standard in industrial instrumentation.

Why 4-20mA?

  • 4mA = 0% (minimum) — allows checking for wiring faults (0mA = broken wire)
  • 20mA = 100% (maximum) — represents full-scale process value
  • Current signals are immune to wire resistance over long distances
  • Works with 2-wire and 4-wire transmitters

How to Scale a 4-20mA Signal:

Formula: Process Value = (mA - 4) / 16 × (Range)

Example: A pressure transmitter has a range of 0 to 100 psi. If it outputs 12mA:

Pressure = (12 - 4) / 16 × 100 = 50 psi

✅ Quick Tip: 4mA = 0%, 8mA = 25%, 12mA = 50%, 16mA = 75%, 20mA = 100%.
