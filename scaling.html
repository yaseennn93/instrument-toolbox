Instrument Scaling Calculator

Convert between 4-20mA signals, percentages, and process variables. Includes automatic calibration point generator.

Essential Calculator

📊 Calibration Point Generator

Enter your transmitter's range to automatically generate 5-point calibration values (0%, 25%, 50%, 75%, 100%).

💡 How to use: Enter the LRV (value at 4mA) and URV (value at 20mA). The calculator will show:
  • 📊 Span: URV - LRV
  • 📈 5 calibration points at 0%, 25%, 50%, 75%, and 100% of span
  • 🔁 Each point shows mA output and process value

📊 Calculator 1: mA to Percentage

50.00 %
💡 Formula: % = (mA - 4) / 16 × 100

📊 Calculator 2: Percentage to mA

12.00 mA
💡 Formula: mA = (% × 16 / 100) + 4

📊 Calculator 3: Process Value to mA

12.00 mA
💡 Formula: mA = ((PV - LRV) / (URV - LRV) × 16) + 4

📊 Calculator 4: mA to Process Value

50.00 Engineering Units
💡 Formula: PV = ((mA - 4) / 16 × (URV - LRV)) + LRV
